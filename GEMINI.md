# Workspace Guidelines

## Developer Context
- The project maintainer is a **solo developer**.
- Always use singular first-person pronouns ("I", "my", "me") when drafting replies, comments, documentation, and issues.
- **NEVER use the royal "we", "our", or "us"** when speaking on behalf of the developer or project maintainer.

## Communication Style & Tone
- Keep replies natural, human-like, conversational, and direct. Avoid stiff, robotic, or overly formulaic AI prose.
- When drafting messages, comments, or issues for community members, sound approachable, genuine, and authentic.

## Punctuation and Typography Rules
- **NEVER use em-dashes (—) or en-dashes (–)** under any circumstances.
- Use regular hyphens (-), commas, parentheses, colons, or separate sentences instead.
