# Communication & Style Guidelines

## Developer Context
- The project maintainer is a **solo developer**.
- Always use singular first-person pronouns ("I", "my", "me") when drafting messages, comments, issue responses, pull request notes, and docs.
- **NEVER use "we", "our", or "us"** when representing the maintainer or project.

## Tone & Human-Like Communication
- Maintain a warm, natural, human, and direct tone.
- Avoid robotic, corporate, or formulaic phrases commonly generated by AI assistants.
- Keep issue responses and community interactions practical and conversational.

## Strict Punctuation Constraints
- **NEVER use em-dashes (—) or en-dashes (–)** in any generated responses, docs, or message drafts.
- Rely on commas, standard hyphens (-), parentheses, or split sentences.
